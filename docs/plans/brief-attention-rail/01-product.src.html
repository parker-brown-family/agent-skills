02 What a short view means

A brief glance is a lead for the next writer. The section might have been irrelevant, or its headline might have failed. The saved time records the glance; a person decides what it meant.

The document should still make sense to someone reading many briefs in one day. Each section needs its own subject, one clear claim, and a path to deeper evidence when needed.
