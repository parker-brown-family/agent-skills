# Register contracts

A register is one finished telling of the finding. The property profile names
which axis is in use; the contract shape is the same either way — length, job,
voice, what it opens with, what's allowed, what's banned, how it ends.

## Choosing the axis

This is the diagnostic question, and it belongs in the intake:

> **Do readers arrive differing in what they already know, or in how much they
> want right now?**

- **Differing in knowledge → the expertise axis.** A beginner and an advanced
  runner need *different protocols*, not one protocol in two voices. Splitting
  by appetite would hand the beginner advice they can't use safely.
- **Differing in appetite → the treatment axis.** Everyone needs the same
  finding; some want it in fifteen seconds, some want the derivation. Splitting
  by expertise would imply the short one is for beginners, which insults the
  busy expert who chose it.

Get this wrong and every article on the property fights its own tab bar.

## Axis A — treatment (tl;dr · ELI5 · technical)

Monotonic in **commitment**, not difficulty. ELI5 is longer than tl;dr and
simpler than both, so the order is a promise about length, not a ramp in
sophistication. Default register: **tl;dr** — the reader lands on the hook.

| | tl;dr | ELI5 | technical |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 120–200 words | 500–900 words | no cap |
| **Job** | Deliver the finding. Earn the click to the others. | Make a stranger care, and hand them the vocabulary without them noticing. | Let a peer check the work and attack it. |
| **Voice** | Compressed narrative. No preamble. | One governing metaphor, held to the end. Concrete nouns. | Plain and exact. Numbers over adjectives. |
| **Opens with** | *The one mistake / number / idea* — a single sentence | *The picture to hold* — the metaphor, stated once | *The claim* — falsifiable, in one sentence |
| **Allowed** | One figure at most. Links out. | Figures, drawings, scenes. Real terms in parentheses, once each. | Tables, code, equations, citations, stat strips, comparison cards. |
| **Banned** | Citations, tables, subheadings, throat-clearing. | Citations, tables, equations, jargon before its plain-language version. | Nothing — but every number carries a source. |
| **Ends with** | A pointer to the other registers. | The reader holding the real terms. | What would falsify it. |
| **Required** | **Always** — it's the landing register | Optional | Optional |

### Writing the tl;dr

It is not a summary. A summary of prose reads like a summary — flat, hedged,
and obviously downstream of something better. The tl;dr is written *from the
finding*, in past tense, as the shortest complete telling that still has a turn
in it. If it has no turn, it's an abstract.

Cut every sentence that sets up rather than says. The reader arrived here; they
do not need to be walked to the door.

### Writing the ELI5

One metaphor, chosen before the first sentence and held to the last. **If the
metaphor needs a second metaphor to survive, it's the wrong metaphor** — go back
and pick again rather than stacking them.

Scene, then complication, then the turn, then what it means. No citations: a
superscript breaks the spell, and the article-level sources list already covers
it. When a real term has to appear, it arrives in parentheses after the plain
version, once, and is never explained twice.

The test: a reader who finishes the ELI5 can use the real vocabulary in a
sentence, and can't point to where they learned it.

### Writing the technical

The register that has to survive a hostile reader. Numbers with sources,
mechanisms rather than assertions, and an explicit statement of what would
falsify the finding — the strongest thing in a technical piece is usually the
paragraph inviting the attack.

Sensory detail is decoration here. Replace it with magnitude.

## Axis B — expertise (beginner · intermediate · advanced)

Monotonic in **difficulty**. Default register: **beginner**. Each level is
written for a reader who will act on it, so the split is about what is *safe and
useful to do* at that level, not about vocabulary.

| | beginner | intermediate | advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 400–800 words | 800–1,500 words | no cap |
| **Job** | One rule they can follow tomorrow, safely. | The protocol, and which decisions are theirs. | The mechanism, the numbers, and the edge cases. |
| **Voice** | Direct, warm, second person. | Practical, specific, still second person. | Peer-to-peer. Precise. |
| **Opens with** | *The one rule* — a single instruction | *The protocol* — what to do, in order | *The claim* — the mechanism being asserted |
| **Allowed** | Plain analogies, one scale or table. | Tables, protocols, dosing, decision points. | Physiology, citations, equations, contraindications. |
| **Banned** | Jargon, hedged ranges, anything requiring equipment not named. | Unsourced numbers. | Nothing. |
| **Ends with** | The bottom line, restated as an action. | What to change first, and what to leave alone. | What would falsify it, and where the guidance stops. |
| **Required** | **Always** — it's the landing register | Optional | Optional |

**The safety floor is not a register.** Where the property profile declares a
liability gate, the warning appears in *every* register, in full, and is never
softened for the beginner or assumed for the advanced.

## Rules that hold on both axes

- **Each register gets its own headline**, written for its voice. Not the H1
  repeated, and not the H1 with a suffix.
- **Each register opens with exactly one lead callout** — the one rule, the one
  idea, the claim, the picture to hold. One. A second callout in the opening
  position means neither is the point.
- **No register may defer to another.** "For the real explanation see the
  technical tab" is an admission that this register isn't finished.
- **The default register carries the finding**, because it's what a reader who
  reads nothing else will take away.
- **Registers are optional except the default.** Three registers per article
  triples the cost, most articles won't earn it, and a padded register is worse
  than an absent one. If only the default exists, the tab bar doesn't render.
