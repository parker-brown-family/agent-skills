# New Article Human SEO/GEO Handoff

Use this reference when `/improve-seo-geo` is run for a newly published article, guide, recipe, blog post, or other editorial page. Produce owner instructions that are concrete enough for a non-technical person to execute after the code work lands.

## Fill In First

- Article title:
- Canonical article URL:
- Primary service, product, category, or contact page:
- Related pillar page:
- Best existing page to link back from:
- Local-business profile URL, if relevant:
- Next supporting article or page:

## Owner Steps

Tell the owner to:

1. Commit, push, and deploy the current site changes, unless the agent already did this.
2. After deployment, open and confirm these URLs work publicly:
   - `/robots.txt`
   - `/sitemap.xml`
   - the canonical article URL
   - the primary service, product, category, or contact page
3. In Google Search Console, submit the canonical sitemap URL.
4. In Google Search Console -> URL Inspection, request indexing for:
   - homepage
   - the canonical article URL
   - the primary linked service/product/category page
   - contact or booking page, when relevant
   - the related pillar page or strongest internal-link source
5. Run Google Rich Results Test on the canonical article URL.
6. If local-business relevant, confirm business profile details match the website exactly:
   - business name
   - location or service area
   - phone
   - booking/contact URL
   - hours, if visible on the site
7. Publish a Google Business Profile update post when relevant.
8. Share the article on owned social/newsletter channels with the canonical URL.
9. Write or queue the next supporting article/page, then link it back to the new article and the primary service/category page after it is live.

## Google Business Profile Post Template

Use only facts visible in the article or provided by the owner.

```text
New guide: [plain-language article topic]. [Business/person name] shares practical guidance for [target audience] working on [main problem, goal, condition, place, or event].
```

Recommended CTA:

```text
Learn more
```

Recommended link:

```text
[canonical article URL]
```

Avoid guaranteed results, exaggerated medical claims, fake urgency, review requests inside the post, or details that are not on the site.

## Social And Newsletter Templates

Instagram:

```text
[Short hook question?]

[One or two sentences about the problem the article helps with.]

I put together a guide covering [two or three practical points].

Link in bio.
```

Newsletter:

```text
I published a new guide on [article topic]. It covers [two or three practical points] and links back to related support for [audience or location].
```

## Follow-Up Content Cluster

Suggest 4-6 supporting ideas. Each idea should include:

- working title
- search intent
- page it should link back to
- service/product/category/contact page it should also link to

For a single immediate next step, recommend the one supporting article that most naturally answers the next question a reader would ask after reading the new article.
