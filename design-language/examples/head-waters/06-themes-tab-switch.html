Head Waters and Piper · design language, round 3 · 2026-11-18

Colour from Omarchy themes, one folio tab, one theme switch

Each product takes its colour from an Omarchy theme by one rule. Every app gets one folio tab, a 2 px line, a switch remembered with the account, and two open-source faces. What you ticked is unchanged; feel marks what is for Sol.

Waiting on Parker

The palette, after Sol's review. The single consent tick on the card page. Silo opening on Today. Nothing here is built into an app yet; the tokens are version 3, and what breaks is in 05-changes-for-builders-v3.md.

01 Themes

The theme gives the hue, the product's intent its strength, and lightness moves only as far as a check needs. Neutrals take a trace of the theme's ground.

  1. Retro 82's accent is orange, a status hue, so Surge takes the azure between its navy and teal feel. Praus's gold is Scythe's tab bar, never a fill: gold is the warning hue.
  2. Strength: black and white (Scythe), grey (Stone), gentle (Silo, Home), bright (Surge) feel.
  3. Lightness moved six times to pass the checks, each recorded in tokens.json.
  4. Home is inferred: it already wore olive, and Miasma is Omarchy's olive feel.

02 Piper dark

Piper's dark mode is its brand sheet's "logo on green field": green ground, parchment ink, lavender for what is active, burgundy for the reed, gold for the wordmark.

03 Folio tab and line

The selected item opens into the page: a 3 px bar over its words in the product's colour, a 10 px chip at the right, 4 px rounding at the left (a third of a card's). The line is the edge colour.

04 Theme switch

One button on every bar, copied from the Terminal Delight docs' switch with a third state added: system, light, dark.

  1. Remembered with the account, on every device and in every app.
  2. No flash: a shared-domain cookie mirrors it, the server renders it, and system is decided in the page head before paint.
  3. Signed out: this device's choice, else system; the switch is in the footer.

05 Type

Deco sets no face of its own: its chrome is Terminal Delight's default, JetBrains Mono, with tracked capitals for labels. So titles, tabs and numbers take JetBrains Mono; reading and typing take Inter. Fraunces goes.

06 Marks and Tailwind

Marks are placeholders, labelled so. Tailwind stays: three apps already run Tailwind 4, and one @theme block turns these tokens into utilities, so nobody writes a hex. The tokens remain plain CSS variables, so Piper's native theme reads the same values.
